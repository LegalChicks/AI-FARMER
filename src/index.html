    Synchronization remedials

    Correct from biology, not the date

    P LATE

    First check water, stand, pest pressure, final leaf number, and PI. A general manual’s emergency urea rate is not M20-validated; trial any authorized correction on a marked strip before broad use.

    P EARLY

    Do not chase it with a high S-line nitrogen dose. Recalculate overlap from heading counts and use the P-line’s longer flowering duration where adequate.

    GATE

    At D54–D60, record final leaves and dissect primordia in both parents. P2/P3 remain labelled reserves unless evidence and the production supervisor authorize them.

    Research register

    What is known, modelled, and still missing

    Documented

    Parent identity and stagger

    PRUP TG102 is the TGMS S-line; TG102M is the pollen parent. PhilRice’s M20 work supports a primary P sowing five days after S.

    Documented

    Flowering behavior

    Public M20 guidance reports about six flowering days for S and ten for P, with the opening peak near 10:00.

    Modelled

    Sterility window

    D60–D88 is a conservative union based on the public 85–102 DAS S-line heading range and a 25–14 day pre-heading sensitive interval.

    Protocol-controlled

    Product doses

    No complete public current M20 schedule gives the four N split quantities, GA₃ commercial dose, or pesticide products. The signed breeder/contract protocol, field diagnosis, and current label must complete them.
